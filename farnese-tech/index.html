Privacy Policy — Farnese Service App

How the Farnese Service App handles information, what it sends to our servers, and what it deliberately does not do.

Effective: 18 November 2026  ·  Applies to: Farnese Service App for iOS and Android (com.farnese.serviceapp)  ·  Operated by: Farnese Australia Pty Ltd

1. About this app and this policy

The Farnese Service App is a working tool for Farnese field service technicians, operated by Farnese Australia Pty Ltd. Technicians use it to run service jobs, record labour and parts, look up stock, manage customer and machine records, record customer relationship activity, take job photographs, and capture a customer's signature when a job is completed.

The app is used by Farnese staff. It is not a consumer app. Accounts are issued by Farnese and cannot be created inside the app.

This policy describes what the app itself does. It is written from how the software actually behaves, not from everything an app of this kind might do.

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. Nothing you do in it is tracked across other apps or websites. It reads your location only at the moment you clock in, clock out or check in to a job, and never in the background. It does not access your contacts, calendar, microphone, health data or advertising identifier. It does not build a profile of you, and it does not share information with data brokers.

People appearing in photographs

Job photographs are taken on customer sites, so a photograph may happen to include people, vehicles or registration plates that were in frame. Those photographs are uploaded and kept in the same way as any other. Take the same care you would with any workplace photography.

3. Device permissions

  • Camera: to take job photographs.
  • Location (only while you are using the app): to record where you are when you clock in or out, or check in to or out of a job. Clocking in and out needs it. The app never asks for background location.
  • Notifications: to tell you about jobs and customer cases assigned to you, to remind you to clock in at 6:30am on weekdays and to clock out at 5pm, and to show that you are clocked in. You can decline this, and the app still works.
  • Alarms and reminders (Android, optional): so the clock-in and clock-out reminders arrive at exactly the right time. If you don't allow it, the reminders still come, but Android may deliver them a little late.
  • Photos and storage: on current versions of Android and iOS the app uses the system photo picker and needs no access to your photo library. On older Android versions (12 and earlier) it asks for storage access so it can attach and save job photographs.
  • Internet: to reach our servers. Granted automatically; you are not asked.

The app does not request access to your microphone, contacts or calendar, or to your location in the background.

4. Where information is stored, and who can reach it

Information is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections to the Farnese Inventory System, our business system, where it is stored. It can be reached by Farnese staff whose role gives them access to it, through this app and our other business software.

We do not sell information, and we do not disclose it to anyone else except where we are required to by law.

Service providers and overseas disclosure

  • DigitalOcean hosts our servers and their storage on our behalf, in its Sydney region, in Australia. DigitalOcean is headquartered in the United States, and its staff may reach those systems from outside Australia while operating them.
  • Google delivers push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and provides address suggestions (Google Places).
  • Apple delivers push notifications to iPhones.

Operational and security logs

Our servers keep ordinary technical logs of requests, which may include your IP address, the time of the request and what was requested. They exist to keep the service secure and to investigate faults. They are not used for advertising or profiling.

5. How long information is kept

  • Business records (jobs, customers, machines, photographs, signatures, customer relationship activity): kept for as long as Farnese needs them as business records and as the law requires.
  • Information on the device: until you sign out, clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • Sign-in tokens: they stop working at their expiry time, and signing out erases them from the device.
  • Push notification token: removed from our server when you sign out.

6. Deleting your data and your account

Your account. To have your account closed and the personal information held about you deleted, write to it@farnese.com.au from the address on the account. We will acknowledge the request within 5 business days and act on it within 30 days, or explain why we cannot.

Business records. Jobs, customer records, photographs and signatures are Farnese's business records, not personal records of the technician who entered them. Closing your account does not delete them.

7. Security

  • All communication with our servers uses encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Your password is sent to sign you in and is never written to the device.
  • Sign-in tokens are held in the platform's protected credential store, not in ordinary app storage.

No system is perfectly secure. A phone that is unlocked, shared or compromised may expose whatever is on it, so treat it as you would any other work equipment.

8. Your rights, and complaints

Farnese Australia Pty Ltd handles personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. You can ask us for access to the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to the address in section 11.

If you believe we have mishandled your privacy, write to us at it@farnese.com.au and tell us what happened. We will acknowledge your complaint within 5 business days and give you a written response within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can take the matter to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

9. Children

The app is a workplace tool for Farnese staff. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top.

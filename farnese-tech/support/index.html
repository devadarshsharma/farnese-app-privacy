About the app

The Farnese Service App is a working tool for Farnese field service technicians. It is used to run service jobs, log labour and parts, look up stock, manage customer and machine records, take job photos, capture customer signatures, and clock in and out.

The app is for Farnese staff only. Accounts are issued by Farnese Australia Pty Ltd; there is no way to sign up in the app.

Common questions

  • I can't sign in. Check you're using your Farnese email address. If you've forgotten your password or your account is locked, email it@farnese.com.au.
  • I'm asked to change my password. Some accounts must set a new password on first sign-in. Follow the prompt, then sign in again.
  • I can't see a job. Pull down on the job list to refresh, or use Search to look up any job by reference, customer or serial number.
  • Clock in says it needs my location. Clocking in and out records one location reading. Allow location access for the app in your phone's settings, while using the app.
  • A photo hasn't uploaded. Photos taken without a connection wait on the phone and are sent when you reopen the app with a connection.
  • I'm not getting reminders. Allow notifications for the app in your phone's settings.

Privacy and your data

How the app handles information is set out in the privacy policy. To ask for your account to be closed and your personal information deleted, see deleting your data.
